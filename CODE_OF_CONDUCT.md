# Community Guidelines

BLACKBOX welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people. Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable and safe. For conduct concerns, contact blackboxstudiofr@proton.me.
